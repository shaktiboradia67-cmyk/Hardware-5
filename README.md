# Q1.List all the main components you find inside a desktop PC after opening the cabinet, and write a short description (1-2 lines) for each.
Ans.# 🖥️ Main Components Inside a Desktop PC

After opening a desktop PC cabinet, the following main components can usually be identified:

| #  | Component                      | Short Description                                                                                                                                  |
| -- | ------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1  | 🧠 **CPU (Processor)**         | The main processing unit of the computer. It executes instructions and performs calculations required by applications and the operating system.    |
| 2  | 🟩 **Motherboard**             | The main circuit board that connects and allows communication between the CPU, RAM, storage, GPU, and other hardware.                              |
| 3  | 💾 **RAM (Memory)**            | Provides temporary, high-speed storage for data and programs currently being used by the CPU.                                                      |
| 4  | 🎮 **GPU (Graphics Card)**     | Processes graphics and video output. A dedicated GPU can significantly improve gaming, 3D rendering, and video-editing performance.                |
| 5  | 💿 **Storage Drive (HDD/SSD)** | Stores the operating system, applications, documents, games, and other files permanently.                                                          |
| 6  | 🔌 **Power Supply Unit (PSU)** | Converts electricity from the wall outlet into the appropriate DC voltages required by the computer's components.                                  |
| 7  | ❄️ **CPU Cooler**              | Removes heat generated by the CPU using a heatsink and fan, or sometimes a liquid-cooling system.                                                  |
| 8  | 🌀 **Case Fans**               | Circulate air through the cabinet to bring in cooler air and remove warm air from the system.                                                      |
| 9  | 🔊 **Sound Card**              | Handles audio processing and connections for speakers and headphones. It may be integrated into the motherboard rather than being a separate card. |
| 10 | 🌐 **Network Card (NIC)**      | Provides network connectivity through Ethernet and, depending on the hardware, Wi-Fi. It may be integrated into the motherboard.                   |
| 11 | 🔗 **SATA Cables**             | Connect compatible storage drives such as SATA HDDs and SATA SSDs to the motherboard.                                                              |
| 12 | 🔋 **CMOS Battery**            | A small battery that helps maintain firmware settings such as system configuration and clock information when the PC is powered off.               |
| 13 | 🧩 **PCIe Expansion Cards**    | Optional cards installed in PCIe slots to add functions such as graphics, networking, sound, or other specialized features.                        |
| 14 | 🗄️ **Drive Bays**             | Physical mounting locations inside the cabinet for HDDs, SSDs, optical drives, or other storage devices.                                           |
| 15 | 🔧 **Front-Panel Connectors**  | Small cables connecting the motherboard to the case's power button, reset button, LEDs, and front USB/audio ports.                                 |

## 📌 Summary

The **motherboard** acts as the central connection point, while the **CPU and RAM** handle active processing and data access. The **GPU** handles graphics, the **storage drive** keeps data permanently, and the **PSU and cooling system** provide power and help maintain safe operating temperatures.

# Q4 Create a checklist of safety precautions and tools needed before starting the PC assembly/disassembly process, and explain why each is important.
Ans.# 🛠️ PC Assembly/Disassembly — Safety Checklist

## 🎯 Objective

Before starting PC assembly or disassembly, it is important to prepare the correct tools and follow safety precautions to prevent **electric shock, static-electricity damage, component damage, and accidental injury**.

---

## 🧰 Tools Required

| ✅ Tool                                      | 🔍 Why It Is Important                                                                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 🪛 **Phillips Screwdriver**                 | Used to remove and install most PC case, motherboard, PSU, and drive screws.                                           |
| 🪛 **Flat-Head Screwdriver**                | Useful for certain screws or clips, depending on the PC case and components.                                           |
| 🛡️ **Anti-Static Wrist Strap (ESD Strap)** | Helps reduce the risk of electrostatic discharge damaging sensitive electronic components.                             |
| 🧹 **Compressed Air / Air Blower**          | Helps remove dust from fans, heatsinks, and other components. Avoid using excessive pressure or spinning fans rapidly. |
| 🔦 **Flashlight**                           | Makes small connectors, screws, and motherboard labels easier to see inside the cabinet.                               |
| 🗃️ **Small Screw Container**               | Keeps screws organized and prevents them from getting lost during assembly or disassembly.                             |
| 🧤 **Clean, Dry Workspace**                 | Provides a safe area for handling components and helps prevent contamination or accidental damage.                     |

---

## ⚠️ Safety Precautions

| #      | Safety Precaution                              | Why It Is Important                                                                                          |
| ------ | ---------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1️⃣    | 🔌 **Turn off the PC and disconnect power**    | Prevents electrical hazards and accidental powering of components while working inside the case.             |
| 2️⃣    | ⚡ **Discharge residual power**                 | After unplugging, pressing the power button briefly can help discharge remaining stored power in the system. |
| 3️⃣    | 🛡️ **Prevent static electricity (ESD)**       | Static discharge can damage sensitive components such as RAM, CPUs, GPUs, and motherboards.                  |
| 4️⃣    | 🧤 **Handle components by their edges**        | Reduces the chance of touching contacts or sensitive circuitry with your fingers.                            |
| 5️⃣    | 💧 **Keep liquids away**                       | Liquid spills can cause short circuits and permanently damage electronic components.                         |
| 6️⃣    | 🪛 **Use the correct screwdriver**             | The correct tool reduces the chance of stripping screws or damaging components.                              |
| 7️⃣    | 📸 **Take photos before disconnecting cables** | Photos provide a reference that makes reconnecting cables correctly much easier.                             |
| 8️⃣    | 🏷️ **Label unfamiliar cables**                | Helps prevent incorrect connections during reassembly.                                                       |
| 9️⃣    | 🌀 **Keep cables away from fans**              | Prevents cables from blocking airflow or getting caught in rotating fan blades.                              |
| 🔟     | 📖 **Check the manufacturer's manual**         | Component layouts, connectors, and installation procedures can vary between models.                          |
| 1️⃣1️⃣ | 🚫 **Never force a component**                 | RAM, GPUs, connectors, and other parts should fit correctly without excessive force.                         |
| 1️⃣2️⃣ | 🔋 **Do not open the PSU casing**              | Power supplies can contain components that retain dangerous electrical charge even after being unplugged.    |
| 1️⃣3️⃣ | 🧊 **Allow hot components to cool**            | Prevents burns and makes handling the hardware safer.                                                        |
| 1️⃣4️⃣ | 🧹 **Keep the workspace clean**                | Prevents screws, dust, and other objects from causing problems inside the PC.                                |

---

## 📋 Pre-Work Checklist

Before starting, confirm:

* [ ] 🔌 PC is completely shut down.
* [ ] 🔋 Power cable is disconnected.
* [ ] 🖥️ External peripherals are disconnected.
* [ ] 🛡️ ESD precautions are in place.
* [ ] 🪛 Correct screwdriver is available.
* [ ] 🔦 Workspace has sufficient lighting.
* [ ] 🗃️ Container is available for screws.
* [ ] 📸 Photos/reference of cable connections have been taken.
* [ ] 📖 Manufacturer's manual is available if required.
* [ ] 💧 Workspace is dry and free of liquids.
* [ ] 🧹 Workspace is clean and uncluttered.

---

## 🏁 Conclusion

Proper preparation is an important part of PC assembly and disassembly. Using the correct tools and following safety procedures helps protect both the **person working on the PC and the computer's components**. Taking a few minutes to prepare before starting can prevent avoidable damage and make the reassembly process much easier.
